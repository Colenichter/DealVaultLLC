    Frequently Asked Questions

    Straight Answers, No Fine Print Games

    Is there a cost to submit my buy box or a property? +

    No. Submitting your buy box as an investor, or a property as a wholesaler or agent, costs nothing. Investors pay a finder's fee of approximately 1%–1.5% only if a deal we source actually closes — the exact fee varies by deal, city, and relationship, and is always confirmed upfront.

    How fast will I hear back after submitting? +

    Submissions are reviewed on an ongoing basis as part of our vetting process. Your dedicated advisor will reach out once your buy box or property has been reviewed — sooner for time-sensitive property submissions.

    Does Deal Vault represent the buyer or the seller? +

    Deal Vault operates as a deal-flow and investor-relations network, not a traditional brokerage representing one side of a transaction. Our role is to responsibly match vetted opportunity with vetted capital.

    Will Deal Vault structure a joint venture with me? +

    We don't structure joint ventures. We do build referral relationships — send us deals that fit our network's criteria, and we'll get them in front of qualified buyers.

    What happens with my information after I submit a form? +

    Your submission goes directly to our team and is logged in our internal system so your dedicated advisor has full context. We never sell or share your information with third parties.

    Is the Investment Estimator on this site guaranteed to be accurate? +

    No — it's an estimation tool built on adjustable assumptions and approximate average county tax rates, intended to help you get oriented quickly. Always confirm real numbers with your advisor and a licensed professional before making a decision.
